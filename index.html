01 / The person behind the work

Curiosity is the
starting point.

A builder's mindset, a systems-thinking approach and a fascination with what technology makes possible.

I’m drawn to complicated problems because they often hide opportunities to create something entirely new. My first instinct is to imagine the system that could solve them—and then turn that idea into a concrete experiment.

My work moves between artificial intelligence, software engineering, product thinking and interactive worlds. I enjoy the full arc: understanding a problem, shaping a vision, designing the experience, building the technology and learning from what happens in the real world.

“Think beyond what exists.
Build what should exist.”
How I approach building

Ambition matters. Evidence matters more. I value experimentation, thoughtful engineering, honest iteration and products that are useful beyond the demo.
